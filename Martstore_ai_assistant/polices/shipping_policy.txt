martStore Shipping Policy

1. Standard Shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days after order processing is complete.

2. Express Shipping

Express shipping usually takes 1–2 business days after dispatch.

3. Order Processing

Orders generally require 1–2 business days for processing before dispatch.

4. Order Tracking

Tracking information becomes available after an order has been dispatched.

5. Delayed Deliveries

Delivery dates are estimates and are not guaranteed. If an order exceeds its estimated delivery window, the customer may contact support for an investigation.

6. Delayed Order Refunds

A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund. Refund eligibility must be checked according to the refund policy and the order's actual status.

7. Missing Tracking Information

If tracking information is unavailable after dispatch, the customer should contact support and provide their order ID.

8. Required Information

Customers should provide their order ID when reporting shipping problems so the support assistant can retrieve the correct order record.
