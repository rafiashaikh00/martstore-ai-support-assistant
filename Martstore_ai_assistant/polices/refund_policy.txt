# martStore Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Eligibility
Refund eligibility depends on the order status, delivery date, product condition and applicable return policy.

## 2. Delivered Orders
A delivered order may qualify for a refund if the return request meets the applicable eligibility conditions.

## 3. Orders in Processing
Orders that are still processing are not automatically eligible for a standard return refund. The assistant should check whether cancellation is available under the order-change policy.

## 4. Shipped Orders
Orders that have been shipped require a status and policy review before any refund decision. A shipment cannot be treated as cancelled merely because the customer requests a refund.

## 5. Delayed Shipments
A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund. Support must investigate the delivery issue and apply the relevant policies.

## 6. Refund Processing Time
Once a refund is approved, martStore processes it within 5–7 business days. The payment provider may require additional time before the funds appear in the customer's account.

## 7. Refund Status
The assistant must check the actual database record before claiming that a refund has been issued or completed.

## 8. Refund Decisions
The assistant may explain eligibility based on verified order information and company rules. It must not invent order details, guarantee approval or claim that money has been returned without confirmation.

## 9. Exceptional Cases
Cases involving damaged products, incorrect items or policy exceptions should be referred to support when further investigation is required.

## 10. Required Information
Customers should provide their order ID so the assistant can retrieve the correct order record and evaluate the request.
